About Quiz Application — Empowering Active Learning

QuizFlow is an educational assessment platform designed to help students, educators, and lifelong learners test their knowledge, track their progress, and master key subjects. Users can easily browse categorized quizzes, take timed or practice tests, receive instant scored results with detailed explanations, and review their historical quiz performance over time.

  • Free Access to Curated Quizzes
  • Immediate Analytical Feedback
  • Personalized History & Review
